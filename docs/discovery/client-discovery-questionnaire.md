## 17 · Operator ↔ Owner Economics & Disbursements 🏢🧮  ⭐ HIGHEST PRIORITY

> **Why this section exists:** Atriom today gives owners *oversight + the ability to raise requests*,
> but it does **not** yet model the money flow between Eltizam (operator) and the owner (Val/Jawad):
> management fees, collected-rent remittance, owner statements, or trust accounting.
> If Eltizam earns a fee and pays owners their net, this is a **new module** — we must scope it now.

| # | Question | Why it matters / maps to | Answer |
|---|----------|--------------------------|--------|

| 2 | Does a property have a **single owner or multiple co-owners** (with ownership %)? Can ownership change mid-year? | App has `asset_owner` pivot with `ownership_percentage`, `started_at/ended_at` — confirm real usage | |
| 3 | How is **Eltizam compensated** — % of collected rent, % of gross revenue, fixed monthly fee, or a mix? | Drives a management-fee engine (not built yet) | |
| 4 | Is the management fee charged **on rent only, or on all charges** (service, CAM, utilities)? Before or after VAT? | Fee base definition | |
| 5 | Is the **management fee itself subject to 14% VAT**? Does Eltizam invoice the owner for it? | New owner-billing flow + ETA implications | |
| 6 | Where do **tenant payments land** — Eltizam's account, the owner's account, or a dedicated **trust/escrow account per property**? | Trust accounting / segregation of funds | |
| 7 | After collecting, does Eltizam **remit (disburse) net funds to the owner**? How often (monthly)? | New owner-disbursement / payout module | |
| 8 | What is **deducted before remitting** to the owner? (Management fee, paid expenses, CAM, taxes withheld, reserve top-up) | Defines the owner-statement waterfall | |
| 9 | Do you need an **Owner Statement** per period: opening balance → collections → expenses → fee → net payout → closing? | This is the key new deliverable for owners | |
| 10 | Does Eltizam hold a **reserve / float per property** for expenses? Starting amount and replenishment rule? | Reserve-fund tracking | |
| 11 | Are operating expenses (security, cleaning, utilities) **paid by Eltizam and recharged to the owner**, or paid directly by the owner? | Expense recharge vs pass-through accounting | |
