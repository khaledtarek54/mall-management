# Final sweep — UI/UX and ease of use

> Part of [the final pre-staging sweep](README.md). Three audits: the `/admin` panel (all 48
> resources), the `/portal` + `/api/v1` surfaces, and a **day-in-the-life** pass asking whether an
> operator can actually run a month on this.
>
> Every CRITICAL and HIGH was re-verified by the lead. **Four claims were retired on verification,
> two of them the lead's own** — they are recorded in [§5](#5-retired-claims) because a retired claim
> prevents wasted work.

## 0. The verdict

**The admin panel is unusually disciplined**, and the audit that went looking for a CRITICAL found
none. Exact EN/AR key parity (3,972 admin keys, zero missing in either direction; 3,325 static
`__()` references, zero undefined). A real panel-wide table floor in `App\Support\TableDefaults`. A
gated role→widget registry. Bespoke empty states on 45 of 48 resources. This is better than most
commercial ERPs ship.

**The gaps are of three kinds**, and the third is the one that matters most for go-live:

1. **Navigation and form-layer inconsistencies** — real, cheap, and best fixed by four shared changes
   rather than dozens of per-resource edits ([§1.4](#14-the-leverage-nine-shared-fixes)).
2. **Verification and drill-down** — the four financial statements are dead-end numbers.
3. **Whole workflows that don't exist**: no bulk operations to speak of, no password recovery on
   either surface, and a month-end that depends on the operator remembering things nothing reminds
   them of.

The honest one-line summary: **the money engine is stronger than the month.**

## 1. The `/admin` panel

### 1.1 HIGH — an undeclared 11th nav group silently splits the finance menu

- **Remedy class:** EDIT + conformance gate · **Effort:** S

`AdminPanelProvider.php:132-141` declares **ten** navigation groups. Nine pages return a group named
`"Accounting"` (`ScopesLedgerReport.php:80`, `MonthEndClose.php:116`, `Reports.php:81`,
`ArAgingByType.php:100`, `WeeklySpend.php:61`, `ArAging.php:168`). Filament matches groups **by
label** and sorts an unmatched one at `count(groups)` = 10 — the same sort key as "Leasing".

So Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow and Month-End Close sit in a group
positioned **by accident**, four groups away from the declared "General Ledger" that holds the chart
of accounts and the journals. The accountant's screens are split across two places for no reason
anyone chose.

The provider's own comment records fixing this exact class of bug once before — which is the argument
for a gate rather than another one-off correction.

### 1.2 HIGH — the Facility group's label differs between EN and AR, so the sidebar reorders with the language

- **Remedy class:** BUGFIX · **Effort:** S

Four resources return `admin.facility.group` — `lang/ar:4282` = «المرافق» — while the
panel declares `admin.groups.facility` — `lang/ar:565` = «المرافق والصيانة». Because Filament matches
groups by label, these coincide **in English only**.

**Arabic is the primary language for this operator, and this is invisible to anyone testing in
English.** That combination is why it survived.

### 1.3 HIGH — the rest of the admin list

**`Settings.php:133-142` is the only bare `Tab::make()` in the codebase.** `FormTab` exists precisely
because Filament v4.11.8 has no per-tab error badge. Settings has **27 validated fields across 7
tabs** and a header-only Save — so an invalid VAT rate on tab 5 refuses **invisibly**. The operator
sees a form that won't save and no indication why.

**The four financial statements are dead-end numbers** — no `recordUrl`, no drill-down — even though
`TrialBalance.php:133` already puts `account_id` on every row. The pattern exists elsewhere
(`ArAging.php:244`, `RentRoll.php:248` both drill). This is the direct UX expression of
[03-money-gl §2.16](03-money-gl.md): a figure you cannot open is a figure you cannot verify.

**Dashboard KPI tiles aren't clickable**, including "Outstanding AR" (`MallStats.php:128-178` — six
`Stat::make` with no `->url()`).

**Publishing an internal staff note to the tenant portal is one unconfirmed, unacknowledged click**
(`TenantRequestCommentsRelationManager.php:97-107`). The code's own comment calls it *"a disclosure,
not a cosmetic flag"* — and the icon doesn't even change with the label. This is a
confidentiality-grade action with less friction than deleting a draft.

**A save-time refusal discards the whole form on 38 of 42 Create pages.** Expense, DepositTransaction,
FixedAsset, Invoice and Payment guard posting dates *at the model*, so the `DomainException` fires
during save; only 4 pages catch it and `halt()`. On the other 38 the operator loses everything they
typed. This is the UX half of the posting-date guard work — the guard is right, the recovery isn't.

### 1.4 The leverage: nine shared fixes

**This is the most important section of this file.** 48 resources means a shared concern beats 48
edits, and the project already has the idiom for it.

| # | Shared fix | Kills |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A navigation conformance gate** — every `getNavigationGroup()` must return a declared group key | 2 HIGH + 1 MEDIUM at once (§1.1, §1.2, the 8 nav sort collisions) |
| 2 | **Ban bare `Tab::make()`** with a test; require `FormTab` | §1.3 Settings, and the next one |
| 3 | **A `CatchesRefusals` trait** for Create/Edit pages | the 38 discard-the-form pages |
| 4 | **A `FormDefaults` sibling to `TableDefaults`** | three inconsistencies in one place: `M j, Y` in forms vs `d/m/Y` in 71 columns; 22 OS-native date pickers (including `posting_date` and `cleared_on`); and the `‏1,234.50 ج.م.‏` vs `EGP 1,234` currency split |
| 5 | Row-action grouping (Invoices shows ~13 ungrouped) | the widest tables |
| 6 | A shared confirmation-copy helper | the 20 confirmations that say only "Are you sure?" — including **Close period**, **Finalise statement**, **Dispose asset** |
| 7 | Extend the nav-badge pattern past the current 8/48, **cached** | discoverability + [04 §perf](04-architecture-ops.md) |
| 8 | A record-hub pattern (only 3/48 today; the UX-01 Lease hub is still unbuilt) | navigation depth |
| 9 | Route the remaining hardcoded strings through `lang/` | the 10 exporter/importer toasts, 4 English option maps, 7 literal placeholders |

Fixes 1–4 are the ones I would take before staging. Together they are perhaps a day, and they close
both HIGHs plus the worst of the MEDIUM tail.

### 1.5 MEDIUM and LOW

## 2. The tenant portal and mobile API

### 2.1 CRITICAL — no password recovery works on either surface

- **Remedy class:** BUGFIX · **Effort:** S · **Verified:** yes, every link

Covered in full in [04-architecture-ops §Security](04-architecture-ops.md). In UX terms:
[PortalPanelProvider.php:38](../../app/Providers/Filament/PortalPanelProvider.php#L38) calls
`->passwordReset()` without `->authPasswordBroker('tenant_users')`, so the portal resolves resets
against `App\Models\User` — **a `TenantUser` can never reset their password.** The feature that exists
to remove the operator round-trip *is* the operator round-trip.

The mobile path is broken too: `config/app.php:59` falls back to `APP_URL/reset-password`,
`APP_MOBILE_RESET_URL` is absent from `.env.example`, and **there is no `reset-password` route in
`routes/web.php`** — I enumerated them. So the link 404s.

**At go-live, no tenant on either surface can recover a password.**

### 2.2 HIGH — credentials are hand-delivered in plaintext; there is no invitation flow

`PortalUsersRelationManager` sends nothing (grepped `notify`, `Notification`, `invite`, `Mail`), and
`EditTenant.php:57-65` renders the generated mobile password into a **persistent toast for the
operator to copy**. No verification, no expiry, no forced change at first login. Yardi Commercial
Café, Entrata and Mallcomm all send an activation link.

Read with [01-yardi-parity §3.1](01-yardi-parity.md#31-critical--every-wizard-created-tenant-gets-the-mobile-password-password) —
where the wizard's default password is the literal string `password` — the whole tenant-credential
story needs one deliberate pass, not five patches.

### 2.3 HIGH — invoices settled by the three non-payment channels are unexplained to the tenant

There is no credit-note resource in the portal (the API **has** one), **no deposit surface at all**
(grepped `DepositTransaction`/`DepositApplication` across `app/Filament/Portal` and `app/Http` — zero
hits), and no tenant-credit surface. `InvoiceInfolist.php:47-58` deliberately omits `paid_amount`.

So a tenant whose invoice was settled by a credit note, applied credit or a netted deposit sees
**Balance 0 with no payment and no document explaining it.** Three of the system's four settlement
channels are invisible to the party they settle.

### 2.4 HIGH — the published API contract declares no authentication

I parsed `docs/api/openapi.json` directly: **52 operations, 0 declaring security**, no
`securitySchemes`, no top-level `security`, `servers[0].url` = `https://mall-management.test/api` (the
Herd dev host), `info.version` = `0.0.1`, and **403 documented zero times** despite
`EnsureTenantActive.php:25` returning it on every authenticated route.

This is the document a mobile developer builds against. *(Route count corrected: **52**, not the 38 in
the docs — the spec agrees at 52.)*

### 2.6 MEDIUM

`/me/summary` **double-counts credit notes** (`SummaryController.php:32` vs `:40-41` —
`outstandingBalance()` already nets them) · the overdue figure counts `status='overdue'`, a column
`Tenant.php:461-465` documents as untrustworthy and which `Invoice.php:560-569` never sets on a
part-paid arrear · 429 responses drop `Retry-After`/`X-RateLimit-*` (`bootstrap/app.php:144-158`
rebuilds the response without `$e->getHeaders()`) · `/me/marketing-posts` publishes `meta.*` as

write** (grepped `idempot`, `Idempotency-Key`, `client_token`, `request_id`, `dedup`), so a retried

`FCM_*` are absent from `.env.example`, so a silent no-op is the failure mode · uploads are 50 MB
all-or-nothing with no resume · the reset email is hardcoded English in a codebase at exact AR/EN key
parity.

## 3. Can an operator run a month?

### 3.1 The month-end, as the code actually supports it

Billing has a **real dry-run** (`BillingRunPreview`), the close has a **real state-aware checklist**
(`MonthEndClose`), AR reporting is deep and CSV-exportable, and every scan is idempotent and
lock-safe. That is a genuinely good spine.

What the operator must remember, because nothing reminds them:

- **`billing:reconcile` is not scheduled** and its discrepancy detail is CLI-only — so the one "do the
  books tie out?" answer is a command someone has to think of running.
- **No monthly trial balance, P&L or balance sheet** ([03-money-gl §2.15](03-money-gl.md)) — the close
  produces a pack the statements can't support.
- **Scheduled-job failure is invisible in-app**: zero `->onFailure()`/`emailOutputOnFailure` across all
  scheduled entries, cron output to `/dev/null`, and no ops widget among the 17 dashboard widgets.
- Four money commands lack `--dry-run` while `docs/modules/19:138` claims all commands have one.

### 3.2 HIGH — the near-total absence of bulk operations

**Only two non-delete bulk actions exist in the entire admin panel** — `downloadPdfBundle` and
`bulkSubmitToEta`, both on invoices. Of the ten high-value bulk operations an operator needs, one
exists (lease escalation, as a scheduled command).

The sharpest instance: **meter readings are entered one at a time and billed one at a time**
(`ReadingsRelationManager.php:220,225`) — roughly **500 interactions a month at 120 meters**.

*(Bulk delete being off project-wide is deliberate and is not counted as a gap.)*

### 3.3 HIGH — delivery is fire-and-forget, and one channel lies

**"Send WhatsApp" is a stub that reports success.**
[InvoicesTable.php:518-528](../../app/Filament/Admin/Resources/Invoices/Tables/InvoicesTable.php#L518-L528) —
the entire `action()` is a `Notification::make()->success()->send()`. Nothing is sent. I verified the
setting is genuinely wired (`AppServiceProvider.php:204` maps
`integrations.whatsapp.enabled` → the `whatsapp_enabled` setting), so the operator **can** switch it
on from `Settings.php:429`, see the button on issued and overdue invoices, click it, and be told the
tenant was chased. **This is the one place in the product that reports a false result**, and it should
be removed or built before staging.

More broadly there is **no invoice delivery tracking** — no `sent_at`/`delivered_at`, no bounce
handling, no resend (`MonthlyBillingService.php:227-243`).

### 3.4 The payables side has no export at all

`ListVendorBills.php:17` and `ListExpenses.php:17` are `CreateAction` alone. The CSV pass that made
the system accountant-workable covered **receivables only** — so AP is PDF-or-nothing, and there is
no AP aging, no cash position and no budget-vs-actual.

### 3.5 Operator mistake → correction path

The good news, verified across the sweep: **cancel, void, credit-note, reverse and un-apply all exist
and all leave a document an auditor can follow.** The never-delete policy is doing its job.

The gaps are narrower and specific: **voiding a monthly invoice permanently blocks re-billing that
lease-month** ([03-money-gl §2.7](03-money-gl.md)) · a **retroactive rent change is never collected**
([01-yardi-parity §1.4](01-yardi-parity.md)) · a **partial write-off can be repeated to exceed the
debt** ([03-money-gl §1.2](03-money-gl.md)) · and **a failed billing run has no runbook**.

## 4. At or above benchmark — do NOT touch

- **EN/AR parity is exact** — 4,153 keys per locale across 6 files, zero drift, 3,325 static `__()`
  references with zero undefined. Verified independently by the lead. This is rare and valuable.
- **`App\Support\TableDefaults`** — no polling anywhere, page size 25, capped at 100, no "all".
- **The role→widget registry** with its conformance gate.
- **45 of 48 resources have bespoke empty states.**
- **Portal read-only enforcement** — every write action double-gated with the predicate named once.
- **The API error contract** — one shape everywhere, `DomainException` → 422 with its real message.
- **`SnakeCaseRequestKeys`** covering all four request bags including files.
- **No money is recomputed anywhere in portal or API code** — swept for arithmetic on
  `total|paid_amount|balance|credit_applied_amount` and for `outstanding|arrears|aging|amount_due`;
  every figure is a stored column or a call into the shared helpers, and the statement PDF is
  literally the admin's service. **This is the invariant holding under pressure.**
- **Mobile responsiveness** — 16px form controls under 767px to defeat iOS zoom, with deliberate RTL
  sizing.
- **The unauthenticated shopper surface** — module-flag 404, hand-written allowlists, one `liveFor()`
  predicate, version+TTL cache.
- **The billing dry-run and the state-aware month-end checklist.**

## 5. Retired claims

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `->money('EGP')` emits Arabic-Indic digits in the AR panel | **False.** Verified against ICU: locale `ar` gives Latin digits. (`ar_EG` would not — but only `ar` ships.) |
| `Sum::make('total')` on the paid/balance columns sums the wrong column | **False**, and one agent did report it as a bug. The lead resolved it in the vendor source: `Summarizer::__construct(?string $id)` takes an **id**, and `Sum::summarize(Builder $query, string $attribute)` receives the attribute from the **column**. So `Sum::make('total')` under a `paid_amount` column sums `paid_amount`. It is a misleading id, not a wrong total. |
| "Only 3 of 48 resources have a View page" | **Misleading (lead's own).** All but Disbursements and StockMovements have a read path, almost all via a modal `ViewAction`. |
| "No lease abstract — reachable only from Edit" | **False.** `LeasesTable.php:463` has a `canView()`-gated `ViewAction`; the disabled-form schema is a documented anti-drift decision. |

**Two further lead census corrections:** there are **48 admin resources**, not 55 (55 counted the 7
portal resources, a separate panel), and **17 widgets**, not 1 (that was a `*Widget*.php` filename
artefact).
