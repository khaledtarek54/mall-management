# Glossary

Terms you will meet on screen, in this pack, and in the operator's own vocabulary.

---

## The parties

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | The company running the mall day to day — **Eltizam**. Almost every login you test is theirs. |
| **Owner** / landlord | The company that owns the property — **Jawad**. Receives a statement and a share of the money. |
| **Tenant** | A **retailer** — a company renting a shop. Not an end shopper. |
| **Unit owner** | Someone who **bought** a shop rather than renting it. Pays a monthly assessment, not rent. |
| **Vendor** / contractor | A supplier — cleaning, security, lifts. Has its own portal at `/vendor`. |

---

## Space

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Property / asset** | One mall. |
| **Floor** | A level of the mall. Units sit on floors. |
| **Unit** | A shop. The thing that gets let. |
| **Rentable item** | Space let that is not a shop — a parking bay, kiosk, signage panel, storage. |
| **Area / zone** | A region of the mall (food court, back-of-house) used to route maintenance. |
| **GLA** | Gross Leasable Area — the m² that can be let. The denominator of most share calculations. |
| **Occupancy** | How much of the leasable area is let. |
| **Vacant / occupied / reserved** | A unit's state. **Derived from its leases** — never typed. |

---

## The contract

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Lease** | The contract. Fixes rent, service charge, term, deposit, escalation. |
| **Charge** | One recurring line on a lease — base rent, service charge, marketing levy, parking. |
| **Base rent** | The rent for the space itself. **VAT-exempt** in this catalogue. |
| **Service charge** | The monthly estimate for shared running costs. **Carries 14% VAT.** |
| **Marketing levy** | A contribution to promoting the mall — commonly 5% of base rent. |
| **Escalation** | The clause stepping rent up each anniversary. |
| **Holdover** | A tenant still trading after the lease ended, usually at a **premium** (e.g. 150%). |
| **Renewal** | A **new** lease continuing the relationship. The negotiated rent wins over any rate calculation. |
| **Termination** | Ending a lease early. |
| **Move-out / final account** | The settle-up at the end: what is still owed, what is credited back, what deposit is returned. |
| **Proration** | Pricing a **part month**. This box uses `actual` — amount × days ÷ days in the month. |
| **Security deposit** | Money **held** against the lease. A liability, not income — the operator gives it back. |
| **Option** | A right in the lease — to renew, to expand, to break — with a window to exercise it. |

---

## Money in

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Invoice** | The bill. Starts as a **draft** (invisible to the tenant), becomes **issued**. |
| **Draft** | A working note. Not a document, not in the books, not visible to the tenant. |
| **Issue** | The deliberate act that makes a draft a live document. Needs its own permission. |
| **Payment / receipt** | Money in. Numbered `RCT-…`. |
| **Credit note** | A document that **reduces** what a tenant owes. Never edits the original invoice. |
| **Tenant credit** | Money the tenant already has on account, applied to a bill. |
| **Balance** | What was **owed**: total minus everything that settled it. |
| **Collectable balance** | What is still being **chased** — balance less write-offs. |
| **Write-off** | Recording that a debt will not be collected. Does **not** reduce the balance. |
| **Late fee** | A percentage charged after a grace period. May have a cap and may recur. |
| **Dunning** | The reminders chasing an overdue debt. |
| **AR / accounts receivable** | Money tenants owe. |
| **AR ageing** | The report bucketing debt by how old it is (current, 30, 60, 90+). |
| **PDC — post-dated cheque** | A cheque dated in the future. Very common in Egypt: lodged, then banked on its date. |
| **Pay link** | The link in an invoice email letting a tenant pay with **no login**. |

---

## Recoveries

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **CAM** | Common Area Maintenance — the mall's shared running costs. |
| **Pool** | A year's worth of one category of shared cost, to be shared out. |
| **True-up / reconciliation** | The annual settle-up: actual costs vs what was billed monthly. Under-billed → recovery invoice; over-billed → credit note. |
| **Basis** | How a share is worked out — usually floor area, sometimes occupied area or GLA. |
| **Carve-out** | A cost account or a tenant deliberately excluded from a share calculation. |
| **Percentage rent / turnover rent** | A share of the shop's **sales** above a threshold, on top of base rent. |
| **Declaration** | The tenant's monthly statement of sales. **Locked** once accepted. |
| **صيانة — *siyana*** | "Maintenance". The monthly **service assessment** a unit **owner** pays instead of rent. |

---

## Money out

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Vendor bill** | What the operator owes a supplier. |
| **Expense** | A cost paid directly, without a supplier bill. |
| **Recurring cost** | A cost arriving on a calendar — a retainer, a municipal levy. Bounded by its contract. |
| **Purchase request → order → receipt** | The procurement chain: ask, approve, order, receive. |
| **GRNI** | Goods Received Not Invoiced — received but the supplier's bill has not arrived. |
| **عهدة — *ohda*** | **Custody**: petty cash held by a named person, later accounted for with receipts. |
| **Advance** | Money lent to a staff member, recovered from later payslips. |
| **Disbursement** | Money paid out to the owner. |
| **Withholding tax** | Tax deducted from a supplier's payment and paid to the authority on their behalf. The supplier gets a certificate to claim it. |

---

## The books

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **GL / general ledger** | Where every money movement lands as double-entry bookkeeping. |
| **Journal entry** | One posting. Produced by documents — **never typed**. |
| **Debit / credit** | The two sides. **They must always be exactly equal.** |
| **Chart of accounts** | The numbered tree of accounts everything posts into. |
| **Posting role** | A named slot ("the bank account", "accounts receivable") that code asks for, mapped to a real chart account. |
| **Trial balance** | Every account's balance. Debits total must equal credits total. |
| **P&L / income statement** | Revenue minus costs. **Stops at NOI**, then below-the-line items. |
| **NOI** | **Net Operating Income** — the figure a mall is valued on. Before interest, depreciation and one-off items. |
| **Balance sheet** | What is owned and owed, as at a date. |
| **Cash flow** | Where cash moved — operating, investing, financing. |
| **Accounting period** | A month. Once **closed**, documents dated inside it cannot have their money changed. |
| **Void / reverse** | Undoing a posted document by posting its opposite. The history stands. |
| **Depreciation** | Writing down a fixed asset over its life. |
| **Owner statement** | What the operator sends the owner: income, costs, fee, what is owed. |

---

## Facilities

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Tenant request** | The retailer's channel for a problem or an ask. |
| **Work order** | One maintenance job. A **cost object**: labour + parts + supplier bills. |
| **Service plan** | A recurring schedule (service the lifts monthly) that generates work orders. |
| **Preventive / corrective** | Planned maintenance vs a fix in response to a fault. |
| **Trade** | The kind of work — electrical, HVAC, plumbing. Classifies jobs, plans and equipment; says who can be dispatched. |
| **SLA** | The agreed time to respond and to resolve. Breaching it can penalise a vendor. |
| **PM compliance** | Whether preventive jobs were done on time. Finished **on the due day counts as on time**. |
| **Dispatch** | Sending a contractor to a job. Blocked if their insurance has lapsed. |
| **COI** | Certificate of Insurance. Must be current for a contractor to be dispatched. |
| **Permit to work** | Authorisation for hazardous work (hot work, height), bounded to a time window. |

---

## System words

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Property isolation** | The rule that one mall's data is never visible from another. **The most serious bug class.** |
| **Role** | A named set of permissions. 14 of them. |
| **Screen guide** | The per-screen help button: purpose, steps, rules, and what else moves. |
| **Saved view** | A named set of filters and columns on a list, optionally shared and optionally your default. |
| **Ask Atriom** | The question box on every page. On this box its model layer is off, so it answers from what it retrieved. |
| **Audit trail / activity log** | Who changed what, when. Stores **data**, so it reads correctly in either language. |
| **Soak** | The month-long unattended test running on Nile Gate. **Do not disturb it.** |
