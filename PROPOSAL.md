# Mall Management Platform for Jawad Developments

A property operations system built for the Egyptian market — and a long-term technical partnership.

---

## The reality today

Most Egyptian developers run their properties on Excel, WhatsApp, and email. It works at one property. At three properties it stops working. By the fifth, the operations team is firefighting instead of operating. Cash visibility is reactive, not real-time. ETA e-invoicing is around the corner and Excel won't pass an audit. Tenants call the office to ask what they owe.

You don't need software. You need an operations platform that grows with you.

---

## What we've built

A working mall management system, designed for Haya Walk and ready for everything you build next.

| Capability | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| **Lease & billing engine** | One-click monthly billing across all active leases — minutes, not days |
| **Real-time AR visibility** | Outstanding balances, aging buckets, collection rate — live on the dashboard |
| **Tenant portal** | Tenants log in, see their balance, download invoices and statements |
| **PDF invoices & statements** | Branded, VAT-compliant, Arabic + English |
| **Roles & audit trail** | Who did what, when. Filterable, exportable. Built for governance and ETA readiness |
| **Arabic-first** | Full RTL, EGP, DD/MM/YYYY, Egyptian VAT model — built for here, not translated to here |
| **Lease lifecycle** | Quick lease wizard · Renewals with charge inheritance · Termination with unit release |

This is live. You can use it. Today.
